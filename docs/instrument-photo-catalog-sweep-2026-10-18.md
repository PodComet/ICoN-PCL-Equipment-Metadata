# Illustrated catalog photo sweep — 18 October 2026

The NNCI searchable directory returned 2,329 nanotechnology tool entries. Illustrated source/model catalogs used for this sweep include Nanowerk, NIST CNST NanoFab, DirectIndustry manufacturer presentations, RETSCH, FRITSCH, JEOL, Thermo Fisher, Nanosurf and university instrument catalogs. The NNCI directory is a discovery index; many entries need a linked facility or manufacturer page for photographs.

Added photos to **40 previously photo-missing model/category records**: 37 new external matches (32 exact-model, five explicitly representative family/package/generation images) and three reuses of already sourced exact Inovenso models across their electrospraying categories. Missing-photo records decreased from **459 to 419**, out of 1,079 canonical records. Equipment identities, model counts, prices and Purdue inventory were not changed.

All 40 image assets loaded successfully in browser checks using the same no-referrer image policy as instrument cards; their natural dimensions and visual review are recorded. Each image retains its model/source page, scope, check date and a stable `IMG-EV-` evidence ID. The Evidence register in **Evidence & sources** includes these 40 claims, their M/P context and HTTP verification details. A successful image response checks availability at review time; it does not establish configuration or delivery scope.

Remote photographs remain on their source hosts, with attribution. No redistribution licence is asserted. Some seller images are small regional-product thumbnails; generation and voltage limitations remain explicit. The misleading EUROSTAR 60 control/digital catalog match was rejected. Generic custom-built or unspecified instruments were not assigned guessed photographs.
