P44 is segmented-flow synthesis with inline optical monitoring of cesium lead halide nanocrystals (Lignos, DOI 10.1021/acs.chemmater.9b03438). P45 is repeated phosphorus insertion/extraction to hollow Pd nanocrystals (Tianou, DOI 10.1038/s41467-017-01258-0). Each has a conceptual source sequence with dedicated M slots; probe/bath sonication in P45 remains a labeled OR choice. New input lists preserve uncurated numerical dosages as unknown.

Other findings enrich existing classes and process stages: HAp/carbon nanofiber conversion, shape-controlled gold nanoparticles, aerosol-selected catalysts, graphene-oxide/chitosan nanocarriers, gold dendrite electrodeposition, silver ablation in oil and Ag-decorated silicon microspheres. Characterization-only models have explicit claim scopes. Exact TSI 3085 remains distinct from 3085A. Series-unspecified neMESYS and Flame records do not assert detector, pressure or valve configurations.

Continuous graphene-paper stretching is recorded as a proposed process. It needs a verified continuous stretching class; a roller stretcher is not automatically an existing three-roll mill. Casting, reduction and optional hot pressing are separately indexed from the source.

Supporting-information bibliographies are not comprehensively inventoried. Title-only screening is not evidence that a paper lacks useful equipment.
