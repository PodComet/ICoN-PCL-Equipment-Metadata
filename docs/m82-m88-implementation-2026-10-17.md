## Process links

Fourteen formerly action-only stages now expose a classified equipment slot. Liquid transfer, magnetic separation, DAQ/control integration and powder loading remain optional implementation choices. Water-quench vessels, casting fixtures, and wafer development/lift-off benches have required class slots. Source equipment sequences are preserved verbatim. Specific model performance, chemistry, dimensions and configuration still require review.

M85 models found in this pass are wafer wet benches. They are excluded from P25.5 colloid washing and P31.4 gel solvent exchange; those source actions remain explicit and their compatible station subtypes remain an open gap. M87 microscopy casting moulds are candidate fixtures, not validated P16 scaffold moulds. Passive desiccators are separated from casting fixtures and are not offered for that casting slot; the Dry-Keeper manual excludes volatile/flammable substances. M88 powder dispensers are not demonstrated graphite SPS die-loading systems. A class match and completion animation do not assert a validated procedure.

Subtype constraints govern slot selection, the all-step instrument pool, validation and automatic filling of repeated slots. Existing saved model/configuration data are retained.
