## About the project

The Nanomaterial Equipment Ontology connects research instruments with material families, manufacturing processes, model variants, documentation and price evidence. Researchers can inspect the evidence behind a record, compare equipment and construct conceptual equipment sequences. Process plans describe equipment coverage; they do not execute experiments or establish validated operating procedures, yields or safety limits.

ICoN-PCL is the Intelligent Codesign Cloud Lab for Non-Equilibrium and Low-Dimensional Materials. ## Open-science practice

The [public repository](https://github.com/PodComet/ICoN-PCL-Equipment-Metadata) provides source code, curated CSV catalogs, reference exports, source-linked evidence, review records and reproducible data-building scripts and tests. Curator-approved shared changes can be exported, reviewed and committed. Edits in the website do not automatically create a Git commit.

Each equipment record distinguishes model identity, document scope, photo scope, price evidence and research relationships. Estimates carry an asterisk and their calculation basis. Missing evidence remains explicit. Manufacturer verification is not a performance endorsement. Purdue badges identify the designated equipment list; they do not by themselves establish installed ownership, availability or access.

The planned public service permits anonymous catalog browsing. Signed-in researchers can maintain personal edits and process plans. Shared catalog administration and the private Purdue inventory require an additional administrator session. Private unit identifiers and serial numbers are excluded from the public GitHub seed.
