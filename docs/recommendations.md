# Equipment ontology recommendations

## Recommendation 1 Cold spray category partners

Recorded 2026-10-14. Status: saved for future implementation.

Group **X-CS / Cold spray deposition** with **M23 / Sputter deposition / coating system** under a shared **Coating and surface deposition** browsing group. Include **M48 / Pulsed laser deposition system** in the same broader group. Preserve the individual category identifiers and process labels.

Cold spray accelerates solid powder in a gas stream, and particles bond through impact and deformation. Sputtering ejects atoms from a target for deposition onto a substrate. Their shared coating application supports grouping them for navigation; their distinct mechanisms require separate process classifications. See [TWI cold spray](https://www.twi-global.com/what-we-do/research-and-technology/technologies/coating-and-surface-engineering/cold-spraying) and [Kurt J. Lesker sputtering](https://www.lesker.com/newweb/deposition_sources/technotes_sputtering.cfm?init=skip&section=tech).

When **Aerosol Deposition** is added to the catalog, make it the closest process relative of X-CS. Both use gas-accelerated solid particles to form coatings through impact. Aerosol Deposition has no assigned M identifier in the current catalog. See the [University of Manchester equipment description](https://research.manchester.ac.uk/en/equipments/aerosol-deposition/).

Keep the Purdue P badge and institutional provenance independent of the technical category. Retain cold spray as a separate equipment family from the proposed combined electrospinning and electrospraying family.
