## Controller price and complete-system scope

The [official exact-model page](https://www.keyence.com/products/3d-measure/3d-scanner/vr-6000/models/vr-6000/) identifies **VR-6000 as a controller**, with VR-6100 or VR-6200 measurement heads. The canonical instrument notes already distinguished this from a complete profiler. The existing photograph is a series/system illustration and does not identify a Purdue unit's head or configuration.

[Government Scientific Source](https://www.govsci.com/product-detail/Keyence--Corp/VR-6000/EA/) publishes **USD 11,550 per VR-6000 measurement controller**, inventory KYCVR6000EA. This was added as one **controller-only reference seller offer**. Condition is unspecified and lead time requires confirmation. Head, stage, dedicated PC, software, taxes and freight are not established as included. It is excluded from new-equipment process totals and does not establish complete-system pricing, manufacturer MSRP or an official list-price estimation anchor.

KEYENCE's official public pricing pages provide an inquiry/sign-in path rather than a numeric price. No inquiry was submitted or account created. Used VR-6100 and VR-6200 system listings were recorded as deferred configuration candidates; their prices were not attached to the exact controller record. Surplus controller prices, editorial ranges, customs values and historic procurement values were not promoted to full-system prices.
