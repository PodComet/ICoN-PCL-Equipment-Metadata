# Metals workflows and equipment

Processes P48–P71 extend the ontology to ferrous, aluminum, titanium, nickel, and copper alloy families (N11–N15). Start with the **Processes** tab and choose a P-number. The equipment directory can filter by the same process or by machine class.

P48–P67 follow capabilities published in Purdue MMRL’s equipment directory. P68–P71 cover additional laser-deposition and bound-metal workflows documented by manufacturers. The step order describes a planning workflow. Alloy eligibility, qualified settings, utilities, workpiece limits, and acceptance criteria depend on the exact equipment and application. [Purdue MMRL equipment directory](https://engineering.purdue.edu/MMRL/equipment) · [Purdue MMRL biennial report](https://engineering.purdue.edu/MMRL/bi-annual-report-23-25.pdf)

| Processes | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| P48–P52 | Laser powder bed fusion, hybrid powder bed fusion and in-process milling, powder-fed laser deposition, bound metal extrusion and cold spray |
| P53–P56 | Friction stir welding, wire EDM, five-axis machining and three-axis milling |
| P57–P62 | Metallographic preparation, vacuum heat treatment, shot peening, X-ray residual-stress measurement, optical profilometry and immersion ultrasonic inspection |
| P63–P67 | Powder flow and density, particle size and shape, sieving, mechanical alloying and plasma cleaning |
| P68–P71 | Wire-fed laser deposition and three alternate bound-metal process trains |

The [source review](../catalog/mmrl_metals_review_2026-11-15.json) records model or configuration evidence and public installations separately. Candidate selection favors documented Purdue configurations and then identifiable models, with independent research installations where available. The public sources provide too little comparable sales or installed-base evidence for a popularity ranking. Class coverage counts named configurations and clearly scoped product families. Several specialties have fewer than ten documented candidates.

The [equipment catalog](../catalog/equipment.csv) links models to processes and material families. The [equipment I/O table](../catalog/equipment_io.csv) gives material and utility inputs, settings and signals where documented, output states, measuring units or the scope of any remaining unit gap, and manual or specification links. A publicly listed system with no disclosed model number is identified by its facility configuration. Purdue MMRL installation evidence is independent of the original Purdue inventory designation.

For examples of configuration-specific evidence, Purdue documents the Renishaw AM400 build envelope and laser rating, the Cole-Parmer BM-450 clamp capacity and motion rate, and the Proto iXRD Mini detector and mapping configuration.
