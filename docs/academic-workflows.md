# Academic materials research workflows

Processes **P72–P89** cover shared-facility workflows for metals, ceramics, polymers, thin films, and battery research. The steps are editable planning templates. Each run still needs a suitable specimen, instrument configuration, method, and operating limits.

| P-classes | Workflows | Main equipment classes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P72–P73 | SEM with EDS and EBSD grain mapping | M38 SEM, with distinct detector requirements |
| P74–P77 | Tensile, compression, fatigue, and fracture toughness testing | M139 static load frames and M140 dynamic frames |
| P78 | Powder XRD phase identification and quantification | M37 X-ray diffractometers with powder geometry |
| P79–P80 | Alloy melting, casting, and homogenization, plus research rolling | M74 melting and casting, existing furnace classes, and M145 metal rolling mills |
| P81–P82 | Differential scanning calorimetry and thermogravimetric analysis | M141 DSC and M42 TGA |
| P83–P84 | Ceramic powder compaction, tape casting, and firing | M72 presses, M142 wet-film equipment, and suitable existing furnaces |
| P85–P87 | Electrode coating, coin-cell assembly, cycling, and optional impedance | M142 coaters, M67 electrode calenders, M28 gloveboxes, M144 crimpers, M143 cyclers, and M57 potentiostats where configured |
| P88–P89 | Atomic layer deposition and research magnetron sputtering | M22 ALD, M23 research sputter configurations, and optional film metrology |

The [model and source review](../catalog/academic_workflow_review_2026-11-15.json) gives class coverage, identifies reviewed configurations, and links manufacturer or facility documentation. The [equipment I/O table](../catalog/equipment_io.csv) records inputs, outputs, units, and sources. A model may require specific fixtures, sensors, detectors, or process hardware to fit a workflow. Public installation records with undisclosed model numbers use a facility configuration name and flag any missing units.

The **Purdue equipment only** checkbox includes models and configurations documented by a Purdue facility. The evidence link is visible in the corresponding instrument details.
