There are 109 added model records, 95 sourced photographs, nine current published purchase prices, and 15 manufacturer-documented newer-generation or successor links. M90 has nine grid-vitrification models; bulk high-pressure freezers are excluded. QSense E1/Explorer and E4/Analyzer aliases are counted once.

Prices retain manufacturer/seller, configuration, currency, condition, date and source labels. Used/refurbished prices are not adopted as new-equipment budgets. Historical quotations are retained only in the review archive. No current price is inferred from facility hourly rates, accessories, expired listings or generic estimates. New-equipment estimates continue to use the approved MSRP/official-list rules; models without eligible benchmarks remain unpriced.

New manufacturers receive a green star only with official identity and independent research-use evidence. All 109 additions were checked against the exact Purdue make/model list, with no new matches. Serial numbers and private inventory remain excluded from this repository.

The ten added preparation/verification stages are optional. M89 provides a separate two-photon polymer-patterning alternative in P06, without forcing the metal etch/transfer branch or inheriting its documented formulation. M90 selection requires a documented cryo-TEM configuration in M39. XRF and tomography are linked to bulk nanostructured parts within their elemental/resolution limits.

Saved plans gain extension stages when opened while retaining existing step and slot IDs, occurrence configuration, prices and custom dependencies. Manual evidence is still required before positions, fibers and directions are populated.
